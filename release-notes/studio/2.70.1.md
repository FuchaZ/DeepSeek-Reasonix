This release fixes two regressions in 2.70.0: the full workbench covering the session column, and the jump pills landing outside the viewport when the rail is open.

## Fixed

- Entering full width from the docked workbench now reserves the rail's width, so the session column is no longer covered by the page (0c672378c)
- With the rail open the scroller is translated; the back-to-top / jump-to-latest pills now carry that shift and stay inside the viewport instead of landing off-screen at 1280px (99775170f)

## Upgrade notes

- A direct fix for 2.70.0; the installer carries its version: `ReasonixStudio-windows-amd64-2.70.1-installer.exe` (https://github.com/FuchaZ/DeepSeek-Reasonix/releases)
- The portable build is also shipped: `ReasonixStudio-windows-amd64-2.70.1-portable.zip` (https://github.com/FuchaZ/DeepSeek-Reasonix/releases)
- The installer is unsigned, so the first launch needs the system prompt allowed (https://github.com/FuchaZ/DeepSeek-Reasonix/releases)
